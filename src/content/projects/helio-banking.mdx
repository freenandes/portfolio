Helio is a consumer banking app built on a simple thesis: people don't want
*more* finance features, they want to feel calm about their money. Less
dashboard, more clarity.

## Approach

- Reduced the home screen to a single answer — *can I spend this?* — backed by a
  cash-flow forecast rather than a static balance.
- Designed and prototyped the motion language: spring-based, never decorative,
  always communicating state or hierarchy.
- Partnered with engineering on a shared spec so prototypes became the contract.

## Detail

The number you see is never just a number — it carries a forecast, a trend, and
a gentle nudge. We tuned every transition against a reduced-motion baseline so
the calm holds for everyone.
