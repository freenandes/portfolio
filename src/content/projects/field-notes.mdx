Field Notes is an experiment in *explorable explanations*: long-form writing
where the diagrams aren't illustrations bolted on afterwards, they're live and
inline, reacting as you read.

## The idea

Most technical writing forces a context switch — read a paragraph, scroll to a
figure, lose your place. Field Notes keeps the diagram in the same column as the
prose and lets a sentence *drive* it. Hover a term, the chart highlights. Scroll,
the system animates state.

## How it's built

- Authoring in **MDX** so writers stay in prose and drop in components only when
  a concept needs to move.
- A tiny reactive store wires text annotations to canvas state.
- Everything degrades gracefully: with JS off, you get a clean static figure.

The result reads like an essay and behaves like a toy.
